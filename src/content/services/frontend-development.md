The frontend is the part of your software people actually see. If it's slow, confusing, or breaks on a phone, it doesn't matter much what's behind it.

I build interfaces that load fast, work on any screen, and stay easy to change after launch. That could be a five-page site for a local business or a dashboard your team uses all day.

## What I build

- Business websites and landing pages
- Web apps and internal tools: dashboards, admin panels, forms, multi-step workflows
- Rebuilds of sites that have gotten slow, outdated, or hard to update
- Reusable components so your UI stays consistent as it grows

## How I work

- **Start with the workflow.** Before I design anything, I figure out what people are trying to get done and cut the steps that slow them down.
- **Performance and accessibility from day one.** Fast load times, semantic HTML, keyboard support, and readable contrast. It's much cheaper to build these in than to bolt them on later.
- **Use the lightest tool that works.** Not every page needs a heavy JavaScript framework. Less code means faster pages and less to maintain.
- **Leave it in good shape.** Clean, typed, well-organized code, so the next developer (or you) can pick it up without a headache.

## Tools I use

TypeScript, React, Astro, Tailwind CSS, Vite
