The frontend is the part of your software people see. If it's slow, or confusing, or breaks, the beautiful backend logic won't matter much.

I build interfaces that are fast, that *can* work on any screen, and that stay maintainable. That could be a single-page static site or a dashboard your team uses all day.

## What I build

- Static websites and landing pages
- Web apps and internal tools: dashboards, admin panels, forms, complex workflows
- Rebuilds of sites that have become slow, outdated, or unmaintainable
- Reusable components so your UI stays consistent as it grows

## How I work

- **Start with the workflow.** Before I design anything, I figure out what people are trying to accomplish and how to cut unnecessary steps or remove the possibility of state conflicts. This ties in with the backend data model.
- **Performance and accessibility.** Fast load times, semantic HTML, keyboard support, and readable contrast.
- **The proper tools.** Not every page needs a fat JavaScript framework. Less code *can* mean faster pages and less to maintain.
- **Maintainable.** Clean, typed, well-organized code, so the next developer can pick it up without a fuss.

## Tools I use

TypeScript, React, Lit, Astro, Vite
