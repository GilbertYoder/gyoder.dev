A lot of valuable data can be collected from machines, sensors, and equipment, and often never gets integrated into systems where it can be most useful.

I connect that hardware to your cloud infrastructure so you can monitor what's happening, get alerted when something changes, or send commands back to equipment through its existing interfaces. This integration supplements your on-site processes by giving them first-class integration into your other work-flows.

## What I build

- Data collection from sensors and existing equipment
- Real-time monitoring dashboards
- Alerts and notifications when readings or equipment status change
- Integrations between on-site equipment and cloud services
- Local equipment communication using protocols like Modbus, including reading data and sending commands

## How I work

- **On-site when it matters.** Hardware work often means being there. I come to see the equipment and how it's used.
- **Work with what you have.** Integrating through your equipment's existing interfaces instead of replacing it.

## Tools I use

Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Python, MQTT, HTTP, Modbus
